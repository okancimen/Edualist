Who I Am

Hello, I'm Özlem. I spent the first 20 years of my career as an English teacher, department head and administrator at some of Turkey's most prestigious international schools — TED Istanbul College, ENKA Schools and IICS. I learned how school systems work from the inside: which schools look impressive on paper and which ones actually deliver.

Then my life changed. My husband's career took us to Singapore, then Myanmar, France, Azerbaijan and Dubai. I went from being an education professional to being an expat mother — frantically researching the right school for my children in a foreign country. Despite my 20 years of experience, it was overwhelming.

That's how Edualist was born. I founded it to guide Turkish families through international school selection with field-tested knowledge — from both an educator's and a mother's perspective. To date, I've worked with over 100 families across more than 10 countries, primarily in Dubai.

Founder's Promise

"Navigating global education with an educator's mind and a mother's heart."

My coaching isn't about memorizing formulas or drilling exam techniques. It's about empowering expat students to adapt, build confidence, and truly thrive in international school environments — while helping parents stay connected and informed every step of the way.

— Özlem Çimen, Founder of Edualist
