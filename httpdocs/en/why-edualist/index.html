Why Edualist?

Choosing the right international school goes far beyond simply comparing websites and reading brochures. Finding the right place for your child to succeed means considering many different factors.

To make the best decision, we look closely at:

  • The Student: Your child’s unique academic profile, language readiness, learning style, and emotional adaptability.
  • The Academics: The exact curriculum fit (IB, American, British, etc.) and long-term university directions.
  • The Family: Your core expectations, budget requirements, and ideal lifestyle or location in your new city.

The Edualist Advantage

With over 20 years of education experience and the firsthand knowledge of an expat mom who has moved her family across five countries, we know how to help. We turn the stressful process of moving abroad into a clear, simple plan—so your family feels supported and your child starts their new school with confidence.

Our Story & Experience

About the Founder

Özlem Çimen
Education Professional | Parent-Student Coach | Expat Mother

For over 20 years, I served as an English teacher, department head, and school administrator at some of Turkey’s most prestigious private schools, including TED Istanbul College, ENKA Schools, and Istanbul International Community School (IICS). I trained in IB programs and had the privilege of guiding hundreds of students.

But when life took my own family on an international relocation journey, I encountered the other side of the coin: being a parent.

When it was time to choose schools for my own children, despite all my experience in education, I found myself completely overwhelmed. Questions like "Where do I start? How do the applications work? How will my children adapt to this new system?" kept me awake at night — just as they might be doing for you right now.

Experience-Backed Confidence

Throughout our international relocation journey, my children and I gained educational experience across different countries, cultures, and school systems. My daughter began her education at the age of four at Singapore International School and later continued in the French education system across four different countries. During our time in Baku, following the closure of the French School, she transferred to TISA where she received English-language education through the IB MYP curriculum; she simultaneously continued her French education through homeschooling via CNED, affiliated with the French Ministry of Education. My son began primary school in Myanmar, continued his education at well-established schools in Istanbul and Baku, and is now successfully pursuing the IBDP program at GEMS Dubai American Academy.

Living and studying across five countries taught our family that finding the right school and educational pathway is not a problem to be solved with a one-size-fits-all approach. Every child is different, and finding the right academic environment requires careful guidance, understanding, and a personalized approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Özlem Çimen?

Özlem Çimen is an education professional with over 20 years of experience at TED Istanbul College, ENKA Schools, and IICS. She is an expat mother who raised her children in Singapore, Myanmar, France, Azerbaijan, and Dubai, holding an MBA (USA), a Harvard Graduate School of Education certificate, and IB curriculum expertise.

When and why was Edualist founded?

Edualist was founded to guide Turkish families through international school selection and education transitions globally. The idea was born from Özlem Çimen's own relocation experience — despite her education background, she felt overwhelmed choosing schools for her children during the family's moves abroad.

Which countries does Edualist advise on?

Edualist serves Turkish families relocating to Dubai & UAE, Canada, UK, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Italy, and Singapore — providing school selection and education transition consulting for each destination.

What makes Edualist different?

Edualist is built on lived experience, not theory. Founder Özlem Çimen is both a 20+ year education professional and an expat mother who schooled her children across 5 countries. This dual perspective — educator and parent — helps families not just choose a school, but feel confident entering the process.

How does working with Edualist work?

The first step is a free initial consultation where Özlem evaluates the child's profile, family expectations, and target country. A personalised school list, curriculum recommendation, and process roadmap follow. Service packages: Academic Coaching, Education Relocation Support, and VIP Relocation & Coaching Programme.
