Summary

Sunmarke School opened in Jumeirah Village Triangle in 2016 under Fortes Education (which also runs Regent International School). It is one of a very small number of Dubai schools offering both A-Level and IB Diploma — a genuine advantage for families who haven't yet committed to one university pathway. KHDA rates it Very Good while BSO rates it Outstanding; IB average 36.4 and A-Level 60% A*-A make the academic case clearly. The school's 2,023-student scale and weak Arabic rating are the main cautions.

The Dual Pathway: How A-Level and IB Coexist at Sunmarke

Only a handful of Dubai schools offer both A-Level and IB Diploma — Sunmarke is one of them. This gives families a real choice that can be deferred until after GCSE:

  • A-Level (Y12–Y13): 3–4 subjects, depth-focused, gold standard for UK universities. 2026: 60% A*-A.
  • IB Diploma Programme (Y12–Y13): 6 subjects + ToK + Extended Essay + CAS, broad international framework. 2026: average 36.4.
  • IB Career Programme (Y12–Y13): Blends vocational and academic content — suitable for practically-oriented students.
  • BTEC Level 3 (Y12–Y13): Vocational qualifications recognised for university entry or direct employment.
Coach's note — Does the dual programme actually work? The A-Level cohort (64 students) is much larger than the IB cohort (21 students) — but both show strong results and 100% pass rates. The IB cohort is still relatively small compared to specialist IB schools like DIA; the programme is growing, and the results validate the quality of teaching at both ends.

Coaching Perspective: Who Is This School For?

The families I recommend Sunmarke to have a specific profile: children who haven't yet settled on A-Level or IB, parents who value wellbeing-focused schooling, and families living in JVT, Jumeirah Village Circle, Al Barsha or Dubai Sports City.

The dual pathway is Sunmarke's most distinctive advantage — and it's genuinely rare in Dubai. IB average 36.4 puts it on par with Dubai International Academy (36.3), which is the UAE's specialist full IB continuum school. For families open to either path, Sunmarke's flexibility may be more valuable than DIA's deeper IB specialism.

I'd hesitate for: families where Arabic education is a priority (they should look elsewhere); children who need a smaller school environment; and families where individual university guidance is critical — 2 counsellors for 2,000 students is a structural constraint that deserves a direct conversation with the school. For those profiles, Dubai British School Emirates Hills (small scale, high teacher continuity) or Dubai College (pure A-Level, Y7+ only, highest academic output) are better matches.

Ozlem Cimen — Coach's note

Two things pull at me about Sunmarke: IB 36.4 and A-Level 60% A*-A are genuinely impressive numbers. But a 69/100 parent score and 2 counsellors for 2,000 students are serious questions — especially at university application time. Families who care deeply about individual guidance at sixth form should raise this explicitly in their school visit. Where Sunmarke is genuinely unmatched is the dual pathway: if you haven't decided between A-Level and IB, there are very few schools in Dubai where you don't have to choose at age 11.

What is Sunmarke School Dubai's KHDA rating?

What are the fees at Sunmarke School?

Does Sunmarke School offer both IB and A-Level?

Yes — one of very few Dubai schools to offer both. Y12–Y13 options: A-Level, IB Diploma (DP), IB Career Programme (CP), and BTEC Level 3. Students choose after completing GCSEs.

Why is Sunmarke not KHDA Outstanding?

Is Sunmarke School suitable for families who want both IB and A-Level options?

Yes — this is Sunmarke's defining advantage. You don't need to decide between A-Level and IB when your child enters Year 7; the decision is made after GCSEs based on learning profile and university plans. With an IB average of 36.4 and A-Level 60% A*-A, both pathways deliver strong outcomes.
