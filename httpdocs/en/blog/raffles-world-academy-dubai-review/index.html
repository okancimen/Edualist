Summary

Raffles World Academy (RWA) is one of Dubai's most consistently cited IB schools — a full IB World School in Umm Suqeim 3 with a KHDA Outstanding rating and a track record of strong IB Diploma results. As an independent school rather than part of a large commercial chain, RWA has developed a reputation for a close-knit community feel alongside rigorous academics. For families committed to the IB pathway who prefer a smaller, more personal environment over the resources of a large group school, Raffles is frequently the answer. This review examines what that offer actually delivers, alongside fees, results and comparisons with comparable schools.

KHDA Rating

Raffles World Academy holds a KHDA Outstanding rating. This places it among the 23 schools — out of 209 inspected Dubai private schools — to have reached the top KHDA category in the most recent inspection cycle. See all KHDA Outstanding schools in Dubai.

What Makes Raffles Different

In a market where most prominent IB schools are part of large commercial groups (GEMS, Nord Anglia, Cognita), Raffles stands apart as a genuinely independent school. This independence shapes the experience in tangible ways:

  • Smaller school size: Raffles does not operate at the scale of a 2,000+ student campus. This means smaller year groups, teachers who know more students by name, and a school community where your child is less likely to feel like a number.
  • Community culture: Independent schools tend to develop stronger parent communities — the school's decisions are made for educational reasons rather than group-wide commercial targets.
  • Focused IB identity: Without the distractions of running multiple curriculum programmes, Raffles builds its entire culture around the IB philosophy — inquiry, international-mindedness, balanced learning.

Fee positioning: Raffles sits below GEMS World Academy (~AED 55k–105k) and broadly comparable to Dubai International Academy. For families who want full IB quality without the top-tier price, Raffles represents the most compelling value among Outstanding full IB schools in Dubai.

IB Diploma Results

Raffles World Academy is consistently cited as one of Dubai's stronger IB Diploma performers. While cohort sizes mean results can vary year-to-year more than at larger schools, Raffles regularly produces IB averages above 33 points — with many cohorts exceeding 35. For the most current results, ask the school directly for the past three years' IB Diploma data, including cohort size and percentage achieving 35+ and 40+.

Smaller cohort caveat: At independent schools with smaller Year 12–13 groups, a single strong or weak cohort can shift the average significantly. When comparing IB results, three-year averages are more meaningful than single-year figures. Always ask schools to provide multi-year data.

Our Coaching Perspective: Who Is Raffles Right For?

Raffles World Academy consistently attracts two types of families in our consulting work. The first: families who have researched Dubai's IB landscape thoroughly and concluded that GEMS World Academy feels too large and corporate for what they want. The second: families already living in Jumeirah or Umm Suqeim for whom Raffles is both high-quality and genuinely convenient.

Raffles is a particularly strong choice for:

  • Families who value community over scale — the independent, smaller school character means more individual attention and a tighter-knit parent body.
  • IB-committed families with a mid-to-high budget — Raffles offers comparable IB outcomes to GEMS World Academy at somewhat lower fees.
  • Families in the Jumeirah/Umm Suqeim/Al Barsha corridor — the location is a genuine practical advantage.
  • Children who flourish with more personal teacher relationships — in a smaller school, pastoral care is typically stronger and more individualised.

Admissions

  • Assessment: Age-appropriate entrance assessment at all year groups. Secondary entry includes academic testing.
  • IB background: Students entering MYP from non-IB backgrounds can transition successfully, but expect an adjustment period of approximately one term. The school's admissions team can advise on specific preparation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Raffles World Academy Dubai's KHDA rating?

Raffles World Academy holds a KHDA Outstanding rating — one of only 23 Dubai private schools to reach this level in the most recent inspection cycle.

What curriculum does Raffles World Academy offer?

Raffles is a full IB World School: Primary Years Programme (PYP, ages 3–11), Middle Years Programme (MYP, ages 11–16) and IB Diploma Programme (DP, ages 16–18). No British or American curriculum is offered.

How much are the fees at Raffles World Academy Dubai?

Approximately AED 48,000–90,000 per year depending on year group. Raffles is generally priced below GEMS World Academy while offering comparable IB quality. Verify current fees directly at rafflesworldacademy.com.

How does Raffles compare to GEMS World Academy?

Both are KHDA Outstanding full IB schools. Raffles (Umm Suqeim 3) is independent with a more intimate community feel, typically at somewhat lower fees. GEMS World Academy (Al Barsha South) benefits from larger facilities and GEMS group resources. Both produce strong IB results. The choice often comes down to preferred scale, location and budget.

Where is Raffles World Academy Dubai located?

Raffles World Academy is in Umm Suqeim 3 — a residential area popular with expat families, conveniently located between Jumeirah and Al Barsha.

Is Raffles World Academy the right school for your child?

Choosing between Dubai's IB schools depends on your child's learning style, location and budget. Book a free consultation for personalised guidance on IB school selection.
