Dubai British School Emirates Hills Review 2026: Fees, KHDA and Parent Reviews

Dubai British School review DBS Emirates Hills KHDA Dubai British School fees 2026 Dubai Outstanding British school
Quick Summary — Dubai British School Emirates Hills:
  • KHDA: Outstanding — 6 consecutive years since 2017–18
  • BSO: Outstanding (2021)
  • Curriculum: British (EYFS → GCSE/IGCSE → A-Level + BTEC)
  • Age range: 3–18 (FS1–Y13)
  • Location: Emirates Hills, Dubai (two campuses: Main + Islands)
  • Annual fees: 53,027 – 79,541 AED

  • Founded: 2005
Summary

Dubai British School Emirates Hills (DBS EH) opened in 2005 and has earned an Outstanding KHDA rating at every inspection since 2017. Its most striking statistic is an unmatched 2% teacher turnover rate — one fifth of the Dubai average. That single number explains the most common parent testimonial: genuine teacher continuity and a real community feel. With around 1,190 students, an Emirates Hills location, and a 53–79k AED fee range, DBS EH sits in Dubai's upper-mid premium segment — and it consistently outperforms much more expensive schools on the metrics that matter.

Application fee: 525 AED (non-refundable). Registration fee: 4,000 AED (credited to Term 1). 10% sibling discount from the third child. Transport and extra activities are additional.

Fee-value context: Among Dubai's Outstanding British schools, DBS EH sits at a competitive price point. Dubai College (97–110k AED) and Repton (57–102k AED) both charge significantly more at secondary level. DBS EH also covers FS1 through Y13 under one school — a continuity that Dubai College (Y7+ only) cannot offer. The 18% of parents dissatisfied with fee-value (vs. UAE average 11%) is a flag worth noting, though it likely reflects the school's deliberately modest physical campus rather than any quality deficit.

A-Level 51% A*-A is below Dubai College's 74%, but half of a cohort scoring A* or A at A-Level is a consistently strong outcome. The 90% Distinction* rate at BTEC Level 3 is also striking — students taking the vocational route achieve equally impressive results.

Coaching Perspective: Who Is This School For?

The families I recommend DBS EH to share a specific profile: children who thrive in small-school environments and parents who value teacher continuity and genuine community over prestige or facilities. It's particularly well-suited to children entering from Y3 onwards with solid English, who prefer a personalised learning environment, and whose university ambitions are primarily UK-focused.

The 2% teacher turnover is not just a statistic — it means your child's Year 3 teacher is highly likely to still be at the school in Year 11. That kind of relationship-based continuity is exceptionally rare in Dubai's international school landscape.

I'd hesitate to recommend DBS EH for: families where Arabic education is a priority, students seeking a broad A-Level subject menu or IB Diploma, and competitive athletes who need large-scale sports facilities. For those profiles, consider Dubai International Academy (full IB continuum), Repton Dubai (boarding + wide sports), or Sunmarke School (A-Level + IB Diploma option).

Admissions: What You Need to Know

  • Almost all year groups are full for 2025–26; limited places for 2026–27
  • Islands campus (opened September 2025) created new FS1–Y2 capacity — more accessible for families with young children
  • Application fee: 525 AED (non-refundable)
  • Registration fee: 4,000 AED (credited to Term 1)
  • Sibling discount: 10% from the third child
  • Strategy: Apply at least 12–18 months ahead; apply to three schools simultaneously
Özlem Çimen — Coach's note

I spent a long time asking myself what genuinely differentiates DBS Emirates Hills from other Outstanding schools. The answer isn't the 2% turnover — that's a symptom, not a cause. The real difference is the school's deliberate choice to stay small. 1,190 students, a 20-year-old embedded staff, and a community where parents actually know each other — that combination is rare in Dubai's international school landscape. The downside is the same coin flipped over: getting a place is incredibly hard. If DBS EH is on your list, start your application today.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dubai British School Emirates Hills' KHDA rating?

Outstanding — six consecutive inspections since 2017–18. English, Maths, Science, Teaching Quality, Inclusive Education and Governance are Outstanding; Student Wellbeing is Very Good; Arabic is Acceptable.

What are the fees at Dubai British School?

2025–26: FS–Y2 (Islands) 53,027 AED / Y3–Y6 60,608 AED / Y7–Y11 71,976 AED / Y12–Y13 79,541 AED. Application 525 AED, registration 4,000 AED (credited to Term 1). 10% sibling discount from the third child.

What are Dubai British School's A-Level results?

2024–25: A*-A 51%, A*-B 81%, 100% pass. GCSE: Grade 9 25%, 9–7 67%. Pearson Edexcel "Best in World" awards. 95%+ first-choice university placement. BTEC Level 3: 90% Distinction*.

How hard is it to get a place?

Very hard — almost all year groups are full for 2025–26, with limited places for 2026–27. Islands campus (September 2025) created new FS1–Y2 places. Apply at least 12–18 months in advance.

Why is the teacher turnover so low?

2% is exceptional by Dubai and global standards (Dubai average: 10–15%). The school's strong community culture, small scale, and genuine investment in teacher wellbeing all contribute. Your child's Year 3 teacher will very likely still be there in Year 11.

Does Dubai British School offer IB?

No — curriculum is entirely British: EYFS, UK National Curriculum, GCSE/IGCSE and A-Level + BTEC Level 3. IB Diploma is not offered. If IB is important, consider Dubai International Academy (full IB continuum) or Sunmarke School (A-Level + IB Diploma).
