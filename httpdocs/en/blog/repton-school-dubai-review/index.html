Repton School Dubai Review 2026: Fees, KHDA and Parent Reviews

Repton School Dubai review Repton Dubai fees 2026 Repton KHDA Outstanding Dubai school review Repton parent reviews Dubai Outstanding school
Quick Summary — Repton School Dubai:
  • KHDA: Outstanding — 10 consecutive years (most recent: March 2024)
  • Curriculum: British (FS1 → IGCSE) + IB Diploma / A-Level / IB CP+BTEC (Post-16)
  • Age range: 3–18 (FS1 – Y13)
  • Location: Nad al Sheba, Dubai (1.3 million sq ft campus)
  • Annual fees: AED 57,178 – 102,753 (boarding extra: AED 66,500–72,000)
  • Students: 2,374, 80+ nationalities
  • Teacher turnover: 4% (among Dubai's lowest)
  • Parent rating: 4.1 / 5 (82% recommend, WhichSchoolAdvisor)
Summary

Repton School Dubai, founded in 2007 as the Middle East's first independent British school, consistently ranks among Dubai's elite international schools. Ten consecutive KHDA Outstanding ratings, a 4% teacher turnover rate (far below Dubai's average) and a 4.1/5 parent satisfaction score place it ahead of most competitors. At the same time, the school has a complex history: multiple principal changes, teacher–management legal disputes, and an A-Level programme still finding its feet. This review draws on parent reviews from four independent platforms, KHDA inspection data, and our own coaching perspective.

Application/assessment fee: AED 525. Uniform: AED 2,550–3,400. Transport: AED 6,700–12,650/year. Early full-year payment discount: 10% (5% for new families). Academic and sports scholarships: up to 10% of fees.

Fee comparison: At an average of ~AED 78,500, Repton sits at a similar band to GEMS Wellington (~AED 74,000). However, when you factor in teacher stability (4% vs 22% turnover) and the 1:12 student-teacher ratio, the value proposition is arguably stronger.

Facilities

The 1.3 million sq ft campus — claimed to be the largest in the Middle East — saw significant investment completed in 2025:

  • Repton Sports Village: Full-size rugby and football pitches, 200m and 100m athletics tracks, 1,600m² astro-turf, cricket grounds, swimming pool, indoor sports halls
  • New Performing Arts Centre (2025): 600-seat concert hall, professional music, dance and drama studios — completed after a 15+ year wait
  • Early Childhood Centre: Wonder Library, Imaginarium, Calm Zone
  • Academic facilities: Specialist labs, art studios, technology rooms, two-storey library
  • Boarding houses: Two separate residential buildings for Y7–Y13

Why teacher turnover matters so much: Repton's 4% turnover rate compared to GEMS Wellington's 22% is a significant differentiator. High turnover means your child may have multiple different teachers in a single year — disrupting learning continuity, trust and academic progress. Low turnover means the opposite: teachers know your child, carry context forward, and provide consistent guidance throughout the year.

Our Coaching Perspective: Who Is Repton Right For?

In our experience, Repton is a strong match for academically confident, independent and self-motivated children. Children who can thrive in a large, competitive school culture, embrace British-style rigour and engage with a wide extracurricular programme. At Post-16, the IB Diploma option makes Repton one of Dubai's strongest platforms for students targeting UK or North American universities.

Where we'd be more cautious: students with limited English at entry (Repton's admissions process is competitive), families who prefer a smaller, more intimate school setting, and those living far from Nad al Sheba — a 40+ minute commute in Dubai morning traffic adds up quickly.

Three Things That Set Repton Apart

  • 4% teacher turnover: The single most underrated differentiator. Your child is far more likely to have the same teacher for multiple years at Repton — this translates directly into better learning continuity, trust and academic progress.
  • Genuine boarding option: One of only two schools in Dubai offering full boarding. Practical for families who travel frequently, or for students who need stable continuity during periods of family transition.
  • Wide Post-16 pathway options: IB Diploma, A-Level, and IB CP+BTEC. If your child's direction changes during sixth form, the flexibility to switch pathway within the same school is a real advantage.

Admissions and Entry Requirements

  • Application/assessment fee: AED 525 (VAT inclusive)
  • Assessment: Age-appropriate academic test; Y7+ includes additional interview
  • Waiting list: No fixed deadline — list forms when capacity fills. Contact admissions@reptondubai.org for current availability.
  • Academic expectation: Particularly for Secondary and Post-16 entries, students are expected to be at or near grade-level academic standard
  • Post-16: Previous IGCSE or equivalent results form part of the assessment criteria

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Repton School Dubai's KHDA rating?

Outstanding — for 10 consecutive years as of March 2024. Repton has achieved Outstanding in every KHDA inspection since 2014–15.

How much are the fees at Repton School Dubai?

2026–27 fees range from AED 57,178 (FS1) to AED 102,753 (Post-16 / IB or A-Level). Boarding is an additional AED 66,500–72,000 per year. Uniform, transport and registration fees are charged separately.

Does Repton Dubai offer boarding?

Yes — it's one of only two schools in Dubai offering full boarding. Weekly boarding is AED 66,500/year and full boarding AED 72,000/year for Y7–Y13 (on top of tuition fees).

What are Repton Dubai's IB and A-Level results?

2024 IB Diploma: Average 36 points, 100% pass rate, top score 43/45, 57% scored 35+. IGCSE: 54.9% Grade 9–7 (A*/A), 97% achieved 5+ passes. A-Level (2nd cohort): 68% A*–C, 99% A*–E.

How good is teacher retention at Repton Dubai?

Repton's teacher turnover rate is just 4% — far below the Dubai international school average. Compared to GEMS Wellington's 22% turnover, this means your child is much more likely to benefit from consistent, experienced teachers year on year.

What do parents say about Repton Dubai?

WhichSchoolAdvisor rates it 4.1/5 with 82% recommending the school (116 reviews). Positives: teacher quality and stability, extracurricular programme, facilities, academic outcomes. Concerns: leadership changes, teacher–management disputes, A-Level programme still maturing.

What is the difference between Repton Dubai and Repton Al Barsha?

The main campus (Nad al Sheba) holds KHDA Outstanding; Repton Al Barsha is rated KHDA Good. They operate under different management teams and are not equivalent in quality standards — despite sharing the Repton brand name.

Is Repton School Dubai good for families relocating to Dubai?

Yes — particularly for academically strong children with a decent level of English. EAL support is available, but the admissions assessment is competitive. The British + IB framework is ideal for families with an eye on UK or international university applications.

Is Repton the right school for your child?

The right school depends on your child's academic profile, English level, your family's budget and which part of Dubai you'll be living in. Book a free first consultation for a personalised school shortlist.
