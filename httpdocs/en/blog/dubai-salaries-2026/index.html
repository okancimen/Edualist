Dubai Salaries 2026: Engineering & IT Pay Guide

Summary

How much can engineers and IT professionals earn in Dubai — and is it enough for a family? This guide covers 2026 salary ranges for engineering/construction and technology/IT, the real maths of zero income tax, and the minimum monthly income needed to support a two-child household comfortably. Short answer: a mid-to-senior engineer or developer earning 15,000–22,000 AED/month can live well in Dubai with two children at a good school.

"Dubai pays well, but it costs a lot too." True — but the picture is more nuanced than that. Zero personal income tax means every dirham of your salary is yours. In Turkey, roughly 32–35% of gross pay is deducted before it reaches your account.

This guide answers two questions: what can engineers and developers realistically earn in Dubai, and can that salary support a family with children in school?

Exchange rate note: All AED figures are gross = net (no income tax). TL equivalents use 1 AED = 13.65 TL (October 2026 approximate). Salary ranges are market benchmarks; individual offers vary by sector, company size and negotiation.

1. Why Think Net, Not Gross?

In Turkey, a 100,000 TL gross salary yields roughly 65,000–68,000 TL net after employee social security (14%) and income tax (15–27% bracket). About 32–35% goes to the state.

In Dubai there is no personal income tax. Your offer letter figure is your take-home pay. This means: to match a 100,000 TL Turkish gross salary in real purchasing power, you only need the net equivalent — roughly 66,500 TL worth of AED, or about 4,872 AED/month.

Any Dubai offer above that threshold puts you ahead. Most mid-level engineering and IT roles land well above it.

7. Finding Work: Platforms and Sectors

  • LinkedIn — primary channel for international companies and large corporates. Search "Dubai" + job title.
  • Bayt.com — MENA's largest job platform; strong engineering and IT inventory.
  • GulfTalent — specialist platform for engineering, construction and technical roles.
  • Naukrigulf.com — broad listing database; well-suited to junior and mid-level roles.
  • Company career pages — Emaar, DAMAC, Aldar, Etisalat, du, Emirates NBD and other large employers.
For engineers specifically: Turkish contractors such as STFA, Limak and TAV operate in the region. A Turkish engineering reference can open doors at these firms. For IT roles, code skills are language-agnostic — a strong GitHub profile and English proficiency are what matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an engineer earn in Dubai?

In 2026, a mid-level project engineer earns 12,000–18,000 AED/month and a senior engineer or PM 18,000–28,000 AED/month. All figures are net — no income tax in Dubai.

How much does a software developer earn in Dubai?

Mid-level backend/full-stack: 13,000–20,000 AED/month. Senior developer or tech lead: 20,000–35,000 AED/month. AI/ML and cloud specialists attract the upper end. All tax-free.

What salary do you need to live comfortably with a family of four in Dubai?

Budget scenario (JVC, KHDA Good school): 22,000–26,000 AED/month. Mid-range (JLT, KHDA Very Good): 28,000–34,000 AED/month. With both partners working, mid-range is achievable at mid-level salaries.

How much does zero income tax save compared with Turkey?

Turkey deducts ~33% of gross in taxes and social security. A 250,000 TL gross salary nets ~167,000 TL. The equivalent Dubai salary (18,315 AED) is received in full — roughly 50% more purchasing power with no other change.
