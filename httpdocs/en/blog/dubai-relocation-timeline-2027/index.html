Dubai Relocation Timeline 2027: 7 Steps Turkish Families Must Start Months in Advance

Dubai relocation 2027 moving to Dubai family Dubai school application Dubai visa guide Turkish families Dubai Dubai relocation checklist

You are thinking about moving to Dubai. Maybe there is a job offer on the table; maybe it is still just an idea. Either way, the most important question to answer is: "How far in advance do we need to start?"

Short answer: Much earlier than you think. If you are planning a September 2027 move — and your child is school-age — you are in November 2026 and the clock is already running. KHDA Outstanding schools will close their application windows in January-February 2027.

This guide lays out a 7-step relocation timeline with specific deadlines for each step, the dependencies between them, and the most common mistakes families make. Your child's school is at the centre of this timeline — because in our experience at Edualist, it is the single factor most likely to cause stress when left too late.

Ozlem Cimen — A note from your coach

Over the years I have worked with hundreds of Turkish families relocating to Dubai. The most common thing I hear: "I wish we had started six months earlier." A school place missed, documents not ready, visa approved but no school — or the reverse. This guide was written so that does not happen to your family.

It is November 2026. If you are planning for September 2027, you have roughly 10 months. That sounds comfortable — it is not. For KHDA Outstanding schools, the application window closes in January-February 2027, meaning Steps 1 and 2 are urgent now.

Step 1 — 9–12 Months Before (Now): Research and Decide

When: September–November 2026 (for September 2027 intake)

Even if the relocation decision is not yet firm, the research process should start immediately. The core questions to answer at this stage:

4 Key Decisions Before You Can Proceed

Dubai or Abu Dhabi? The two cities differ meaningfully on rent, school options and lifestyle pace. If you are considering both, the differences are larger than most families expect.
Which neighbourhood? Your child's school will largely determine where you should live. Popular areas for Turkish families with children: Jumeirah (established expat community, close to British schools), Mirdif (family-oriented, more affordable), Arabian Ranches / Springs (quiet, villas, good school access).
Which curriculum? IB, British (GCSE/A-Level) or American (AP)? Each has different university recognition implications for Turkish students. IB and British are the most common choices among Turkish families. See our Dubai Salaries 2026 post for context on what different careers pay.
Financial planning: School fees range from 25,000–90,000 AED per year. Add housing, healthcare, transport and living costs. Without a solid budget model, the move decision itself cannot be made confidently.
Do not skip this step. You cannot apply to a school before choosing one; you cannot choose a neighbourhood without knowing the school; you cannot evaluate a job offer without knowing whether the salary covers your full family budget. Step 1 unlocks everything else.

Step 2 — 8–10 Months Before: Apply to Schools (Most Critical Step)

When: November 2026–January 2027

This is the most time-sensitive step in the entire relocation timeline — and you are inside this window right now.

Applying to a Dubai international school before you arrive is not optional — it is essential. Popular KHDA Outstanding schools — JESS, Repton, Nord Anglia, Kings, GEMS Wellington, Brighton College — routinely have 12–18 month waiting lists. A place cannot exist unless you have applied and been accepted.

Application Timeline by School Type

KHDA Outstanding IB schools (DIA, GEMS World Academy): Waiting lists can stretch to 18 months. For September 2027, the application window may already be closing — check immediately.
KHDA Outstanding British schools (JESS, Repton, Kings, Nord Anglia): November–January is the critical window. Many close to new applications by February.
KHDA Very Good British schools (Brighton College, Safa Community): January–March applications are still possible; acceptance period typically open until April.
Mid-year intake: Use the KHDA portal's "mid-year vacancies" filter to find schools with January intake availability.

Each application carries a non-refundable fee of 500–1,500 AED. Thoroughly assess school fit before paying. Apply to at least 3 schools simultaneously — relying on a single application is risky.

What to do right now: Email the schools you have shortlisted and ask: "Do you have Year X availability for September 2027 and how long is your waiting list?" Their answers will also inform your neighbourhood and curriculum decisions in Step 1.

Step 3 — 6–8 Months Before: Collect Documents from Turkey

When: December 2026–February 2027

Dubai international schools require Turkish academic documents to pass through a specific chain of authentication before they are accepted for enrolment.

  1. 1
    School / Ministry of Education authentication — Obtain the transcript and diploma from the previous school with official stamp and signature. Private schools may also require Ministry of National Education (MoNE) endorsement. Time: 1-2 weeks.
  2. 2
    Certified translation by sworn translator — Documents must be translated into English by a notary-certified sworn translator. Time: 3-5 business days.
  3. 3
    Apostille from Ministry of Foreign Affairs — The apostille stamp is obtained from the Turkish Ministry of Foreign Affairs or Provincial Directorate of National Education. Time: 3-7 business days.
  4. 4
    UAE Consulate attestation — The documents are attested at the UAE Consulate in Ankara or Istanbul. Time: 5-10 business days.

Total time for this chain: approximately 6-8 weeks. Also check that your passport and your child's passport have sufficient validity — at least 6 months for a UAE visa, and preferably 2+ years remaining for the full enrolment process.

Common mistake: "We'll sort the documents once we're in Dubai." These steps must be completed while you are still in Turkey — returning or sending someone is an avoidable cost and delay.

Step 4 — 4–6 Months Before: Job Offer and Visa Processes

When: February–April 2027

To legally live and work in Dubai, you need an employer-sponsored residence visa. Children's school enrolment and most official processes depend on this.

UAE Visa Types for Turkish Professionals

Employment Visa: Most common. Your employer sponsors the visa; typically 2 years, renewable. Processing time: 4-6 weeks.
Dependent Visa (family/spouse/children): Issued once the primary applicant's employment visa is approved. Children need this for school enrolment and Emirates ID.
Golden Visa (10-year residency): Available for investors (AED 2M+), exceptional talent nominees or certain professional categories. Provides employer independence — valuable for senior professionals.
Freelance / Remote Work Visa: Dubai Virtual Work Visa or freelance licence options exist. Note: some schools do not accept dependent enrolment under this visa type — confirm with your school before applying.
Key dependency: Final school enrolment requires an Emirates ID. Emirates ID requires a residence visa. This means: school application → place secured → visa → Emirates ID → final enrolment. Getting the sequence right saves several weeks.

Step 5 — 3–4 Months Before: Choose Your Housing

When: March–May 2027

Renting in Dubai works differently from Turkey in several important ways.

School catchment first. Identify where your child's school is located before shortlisting neighbourhoods. Transport time and sometimes fee bands can vary depending on where you live relative to the school.

Top Dubai Neighbourhoods for Families with Children (2027)

Jumeirah / Al Wasl: Close to JESS, Repton, Kings. Established expat community. 3-bed villa rent: 180,000–280,000 AED/year.
Mirdif / Rashidiya: Family-friendly, more green space, more affordable. 3-bed villa rent: 120,000–180,000 AED/year.
Arabian Ranches / Springs: Quiet, villas, good school access. 3-bed villa rent: 150,000–220,000 AED/year.
JLT / Motor City: Mid-range, Metro access, apartment options. 2-bed apartment rent: 90,000–140,000 AED/year.

Rent payment in Dubai is different. Rent is typically paid annually upfront by cheque (1, 2 or 4 post-dated cheques — fewer cheques means lower rent but more capital needed upfront). Security deposit is 5% of annual rent (furnished) or 10% (unfurnished), returnable on departure. Agency commission is 2-5% one-off.

Step 6 — 1–2 Months Before: Set Up Your Digital Infrastructure

When: June–July 2027

  1. 1
    Emirates ID application — Filed through ICA (Federal Authority for Identity). After a biometrics appointment (fingerprints and photo), the card arrives within 1-3 weeks. Required for children's school enrolment.
  2. 2
    Mandatory health insurance — Dubai law requires health insurance for all employees and their dependants. Confirm whether your employer's plan covers your family. If not, secure a DHA-approved supplementary plan.
  3. 3
    Bank account — Emirates NBD, Mashreq, ADCB and Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank are common choices. Typically requires residence visa, Emirates ID and 3 months of employment contract/statements. Allow 1-3 weeks — start as early as possible.
  4. 4
    Children's vaccination records — Some Dubai schools require vaccinations not mandated in Turkey (meningitis, varicella/chickenpox). Request the school's vaccination list; complete them in Turkey before you leave.

Step 7 — After Arrival (August 2027): School Orientation and Settling In

When: August–September 2027

The physical move is done — but the process is not. This stage is often the most neglected.

School orientation: Most Dubai international schools run a parent and student orientation programme in late August. Attend where possible. Daily routines, teacher expectations, bus routes, cafeteria systems — learning these without first-day chaos is genuinely valuable.

EAL support: Your child's English may not be strong yet — this is completely normal. The majority of Dubai international schools offer EAL (English as an Additional Language) programmes, providing in-class support or individual sessions. The quality of this support varies significantly between schools; ask about it at every school visit and check KHDA inspection reports for how EAL is assessed.

Third Culture Kid (TCK) adjustment: Children who grow up between countries — neither fully Turkish nor fully Emirati — develop a distinctive identity with its own strengths and challenges. The adjustment period typically runs 3-6 months. Active communication, patience and finding a Turkish community in Dubai all help significantly.

Maintaining Turkish: Many families we work with report that children's Turkish weakens noticeably within 2-3 years abroad. Reading Turkish books, attending Turkish community events and committing to Turkish-only conversations at home for at least a few hours a week makes a real difference.

Why Starting This Early Really Matters

Every step in the relocation process is a prerequisite for the next one. A school place cannot be secured without applying; documents cannot be prioritised without a school acceptance; a visa cannot be initiated without a job offer; Emirates ID cannot be issued without a visa; final school enrolment cannot be completed without Emirates ID. These sequential dependencies mean you cannot simply compress the timeline at the end.

Unexpected delays compound this further: passport renewals, public holidays in Turkey, slow school responses, tight housing market, medical clearances. Ten months looks adequate; waiting lists, document chains and bureaucratic processes routinely turn it into a 12+ month process.

The biggest risk: Delaying the school application. A delayed visa can be resolved; housing can be changed; but if no place is available at the school your family wanted, your options narrow significantly. School quality is one of the strongest determinants of whether a Dubai relocation feels worthwhile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should you start planning a Dubai relocation?

For a September intake, start planning at least 9-12 months in advance — no later than September-October of the previous year. The November-January window is critical for KHDA Outstanding school applications. Document preparation (apostille chain) takes 6-8 weeks; visa processing takes 4-6 weeks.

Can you apply to Dubai schools before moving?

Yes — and you must. Dubai international schools accept applications from families who don't yet have UAE residency. However, final enrolment requires the child's Emirates ID and residence visa. Securing a school place before you relocate is essential.

How long does it take to prepare school documents from Turkey?

The Turkish document chain: school/MoE authentication (1-2 weeks) + certified translation (3-5 days) + apostille from Ministry of Foreign Affairs (3-7 days) + UAE Consulate attestation (1-2 weeks). Budget 6-8 weeks total; factor in Turkish public holidays and busy periods.

What is the one-time relocation cost for a family?

For an average Turkish family: visa processing 1,500-3,000 AED, school application fees (3 schools) 1,500-4,500 AED, housing deposit and agency commission (~5-10% of annual rent), furniture removal 5,000-15,000 AED, initial setup 10,000-25,000 AED. Budget 60,000-100,000 AED for the first month.

Do you need a job offer before moving to Dubai?

Technically no — you can enter on a tourist visa and job-search. But children's school enrolment requires a residence visa, which is typically tied to a parent's employer-sponsored visa. In practice, arriving without a job offer significantly complicates both school enrolment and quality of life.
