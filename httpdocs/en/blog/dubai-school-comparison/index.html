Dubai School Comparison 2026: Repton vs GEMS Wellington vs Nord Anglia vs JESS vs DIA

Dubai school comparison Outstanding KHDA schools Repton vs GEMS Wellington best Dubai international school

Every family moving to Dubai asks the same question: "Which school is best?" The honest answer is that there is no single best school — there is the best school for your child's age, your budget, your preferred location and your curriculum preferences.

This guide compares the five schools most frequently asked about by expat families: Repton, GEMS Wellington Al Sufouh, Nord Anglia, JESS and Dubai International Academy. All five hold Outstanding KHDA ratings. The differences lie in what they offer, where they are and who they suit best.

Repton School Dubai

Repton Dubai — affiliated with the UK's Repton School, founded 1557 — opened its Dubai campus in Nad Al Sheba in 2007. It offers Years 1–13 on the British curriculum, with IGCSE at Year 10–11 and A-Level at Years 12–13.

Repton at a glance:
  • KHDA rating: Outstanding
  • Location: Nad Al Sheba 1 — good access from Business Bay, DIFC, Meydan and newer communities
  • Fees: AED 55,000–85,000 depending on year group
  • Boarding: Full and weekly boarding available from Year 7 (AED 66,500–72,000 boarding supplement)
  • Entry assessments: English and maths; competitive from Year 7 upward
  • Turkish families: Active Turkish community; multiple Turkish families per year group in most cohorts

Repton is often described as the most "balanced" choice among the five schools here. It combines academic rigour with a genuine boarding-school culture, strong sports and arts programmes, and a campus that feels less corporate than some larger GEMS schools. Waiting times vary but are generally shorter than DIA or JESS for mid-secondary year groups.

Verdict: Best all-round choice for families who want a proven British curriculum, boarding option, and manageable commute from newer Dubai neighbourhoods. Not ideal if the IB Diploma is a firm requirement.

GEMS Wellington Al Sufouh

GEMS Wellington College Al Sufouh is one of Dubai's oldest and most established international schools, located near the American University of Dubai in Al Sufouh. Like Repton, it follows the British curriculum through IGCSE and A-Level.

GEMS Wellington Al Sufouh at a glance:
  • KHDA rating: Outstanding
  • Location: Al Sufouh — convenient for JBR, Dubai Marina, JLT and Media City
  • Fees: AED 57,000–98,000 depending on year group (highest A-Level fees on this list)
  • Entry: Very competitive; Year 7–9 waitlists are long, often 12+ months
  • Turkish families: Established presence; school is familiar with Turkish expat transitions

Wellington Al Sufouh carries strong name recognition both in Dubai and internationally. Its A-Level results are consistently strong and its university placement record is well-documented. The higher fee ceiling compared to Repton reflects both location and brand.

Verdict: The strongest choice for families based in JBR/Marina/JLT who prioritise name recognition and A-Level outcomes. Budget ceiling is the highest in this comparison. Waitlists for popular year groups can be long.

Nord Anglia International School Dubai

Nord Anglia Dubai, located in Jumeirah Park, is part of the global Nord Anglia Education group and is the only school in this comparison that offers the IB Diploma alongside IGCSE, making it the natural bridge between British curriculum and international qualifications.

Nord Anglia at a glance:
  • KHDA rating: Outstanding
  • Location: Jumeirah Park — good access from JVC, JVT, Jumeirah Village, Discovery Gardens
  • Fees: AED 50,000–92,000 depending on year group
  • IB Diploma pass rate: 95–97%; average score 34–36 points
  • IB Diploma waitlist: 3–12 months depending on year group
  • Turkish language: Available within IB Diploma programme

Nord Anglia's unique selling point for expat families is curriculum flexibility. Students follow the British primary and IGCSE path, then choose between A-Level and IB Diploma at Sixth Form. This avoids the commitment required to enter a full-IB school like DIA from primary school.

Verdict: The best single-school IB Diploma path without committing to full IB from age 3. Particularly strong for families arriving mid-secondary who want to keep the IB Diploma option open. Turkish language available in the DP, which is rare.

Jumeirah English Speaking School (JESS)

JESS — Jumeirah English Speaking School — is one of Dubai's most loved schools for younger children, with campuses in Umm Suqeim (FS1–Y13) and Arabian Ranches (FS1–Y6). The Umm Suqeim campus offers IB Primary Years Programme (PYP), IB Middle Years Programme (MYP) and British A-Levels, but does not offer the IB Diploma.

JESS Umm Suqeim at a glance:
  • KHDA rating: Outstanding
  • Location: Umm Suqeim 3 — central, convenient for Jumeirah, Safa, Barsha
  • Fees: AED 48,000–78,000 — the most affordable Outstanding school in this group
  • IB: PYP (primary) and MYP (secondary) — no IB Diploma
  • Sixth Form: A-Levels
  • Waitlist: Among the longest of this group; can be 18–24 months for FS1–Y3

JESS has a strong community reputation and outstanding EYFS and primary provision. Families who move here specifically for younger children love the school. The absence of the IB Diploma is a common reason families choose JESS for primary and then transfer to Nord Anglia or DIA for secondary.

Verdict: The strongest choice for families with children in Foundation Stage to Year 6. Lowest fees among Outstanding schools in this comparison. If your child is primary age and IB Diploma is a future priority, plan for a secondary transfer.

Dubai International Academy (DIA) Emirates Hills

Dubai International Academy is the flagship full-IB school in Dubai. It offers the complete IB continuum — PYP (age 3–11), MYP (age 11–16) and Diploma Programme (age 16–18) — within a single campus in Emirates Hills.

DIA at a glance:
  • KHDA rating: Outstanding
  • Location: Emirates Hills — convenient for JLT, Jumeirah Islands, The Springs, Meadows
  • Fees: AED 65,000–95,000 — the highest entry point in this comparison
  • IB Diploma pass rate: 96–98%; average score 36–38 points (among Dubai's highest)
  • IB Diploma waitlist: 12–24 months; new admissions from PYP strongly preferred
  • Turkish language: Available as Language B in IB Diploma

DIA is the right choice if and only if the IB Diploma is a firm long-term goal. The school's IB results are among the best in the UAE, but the fees are the highest in this comparison and mid-secondary entry is very difficult — the school strongly favours students who started in PYP.

Verdict: Best choice for families certain about the IB Diploma pathway and who plan to stay in Dubai through Year 12. The highest fees and longest waitlists of the five schools. Not recommended for families uncertain about IB or planning to leave Dubai before secondary years.

What These Comparisons Don't Tell You

A table can compare fees and KHDA ratings but cannot tell you whether your child will thrive in a specific school community. Every Outstanding school in Dubai has a distinct culture. Repton feels like a boarding school even for day students. DIA has a noticeably international, multilingual community. JESS has deep parental involvement and strong alumni roots. GEMS Wellington can feel corporate and large.

Before committing to any school, visit in person, speak to current parents, and — if possible — have your child shadow a student for a day. The school that looks best on paper is not always the one where a specific child does best.

Also remember: all five schools can have year-specific waiting lists. A school that is easy to enter at Year 3 may have a 24-month waitlist at Year 7. Apply early — ideally 12 months ahead of your intended start date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better: Repton or GEMS Wellington Dubai?

Both are Outstanding KHDA schools with British-based curricula and similar fee ranges. Repton (Nad Al Sheba) tends to have a calmer, community feel and slightly shorter waitlists. GEMS Wellington (Al Sufouh) is older, more central and carries strong name recognition. If prestige matters, Wellington; if balance and manageable commute from newer areas, Repton.

Which Dubai school is best for the IB Diploma?

Dubai International Academy (DIA) in Emirates Hills offers the full IB continuum — PYP, MYP and Diploma — with pass rates of 96–98% and average scores of 36–38. Nord Anglia also offers a strong IB Diploma pathway alongside IGCSE. JESS offers IB PYP and MYP but not the Diploma.

What is the cheapest Outstanding school in Dubai on this list?

JESS Umm Suqeim has the lowest starting fees among the five Outstanding schools compared here, from around AED 48,000 per year. Nord Anglia starts from approximately AED 50,000. Remember that fees vary by year group; secondary years cost significantly more than primary.

Is DIA Emirates Hills worth the premium fees?

DIA charges AED 65,000–95,000 per year — the highest on this list. For families committed to the full IB pathway through to Diploma, the investment is justified by exceptional results and the complete curriculum chain. For families undecided about IB or whose child is in primary school, a school like JESS or Repton offers similar KHDA quality at lower cost.

Which school has the shortest waitlist?

Waitlists change constantly and depend on year group. As of late 2026, Nord Anglia and Repton typically have shorter waits (3–9 months for some year groups) compared to JESS and DIA, which can run 12–24 months for popular year groups. GEMS Wellington Al Sufouh waitlists vary widely by year.
