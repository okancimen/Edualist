Jumeirah College Dubai Review 2026: Fees, KHDA and Parent Reviews

Jumeirah College Dubai review Jumeirah College fees 2026 KHDA Outstanding Dubai A-Level school GEMS Jumeirah College parent reviews
Quick Summary — Jumeirah College Dubai:
  • KHDA: Outstanding
  • Group: GEMS Education
  • Curriculum: British National Curriculum + GCSE + A-Level (Year 7–13)
  • Age range: 11–18 (Year 7–Year 13) — No primary
  • Location: Jumeirah / Al Safa, Dubai
  • Annual fees: ~AED 62,000 – 88,000
  • GCSE results: 70–78% A*/A (Grade 9–7)
  • A-Level results: 60–68% A*-A (Dubai's strongest second tier)
  • University destinations: UCL, King's College London, LSE, Cambridge, Manchester
  • Transfer hub: Most popular Sixth Form destination from Safa, JESS and DESS
Summary

Jumeirah College is GEMS Education's academic flagship in Dubai and one of the emirate's strongest A-Level schools. By specialising purely in secondary education — Year 7 to Year 13 — it delivers focused academic outcomes: consistent 60–68% A*-A at A-Level, 70–78% Grade 9–7 at GCSE, and annual placements to Oxbridge, UCL and the Russell Group. In this review I cover the fee table, academic data, parent reviews, the multiple entry point advantage, and how it fits as a natural Sixth Form transfer from Safa Community School.

Parent Reviews

Positives — What Parents Say
  • A-Level results consistently in Dubai's strongest second tier
  • UCL, LSE, King's and Oxbridge placements every year
  • GEMS infrastructure — resources, technology, facilities
  • Year 12 Sixth Form entry available — eases transitions from other schools
  • Jumeirah location in the heart of the expat community
  • Academic expectation is consistent — graduates are well prepared
  • 25+ A-Level subject choices
  • Experienced teaching staff, strong GCSE-to-A-Level transition support
Points to Consider
  • No primary — separate primary school needed until Year 7
  • Large GEMS institution atmosphere — individual attention less intense than Safa or Kings'
  • Some parents report GEMS bureaucracy in administrative processes
  • A-Level Sixth Form is academically demanding — not suited to every profile
  • Student Wellbeing rated Very Good not Outstanding — academic pressure can be high
  • Selective Year 12 entry — weak GCSE results may block access

Coaching Perspective

I recommend Jumeirah College most readily for: students targeting UK universities — particularly UCL, LSE, King's, Imperial or Oxbridge — who haven't secured a Year 7 place at Dubai College or who are planning to enter at Year 10 or Year 12. The Year 12 Sixth Form entry in particular is valuable: strong GCSEs from Safa, JESS or DESS, then transfer to the best available A-Level environment. Jumeirah College is that environment for a wide range of profiles.

Where I'm more cautious: families prioritising social-emotional development equally with academics (Safa or Kings' serve this better), students targeting IB Diploma (Jumeirah College is A-Level only), families wanting a smaller, more personal atmosphere.

Ozlem Cimen — A coach's note

I recommend Jumeirah College most often in this scenario: the student has done well at a solid school (Safa, JESS, DESS), has clear UK university targets, and Dubai College's ultra-selective entry either wasn't secured at Year 7 or wasn't the right fit. In that gap, Jumeirah College is reliable, results-producing, and logistically sensible. One consistent message: the A-Level Sixth Form is genuinely demanding — I recommend it for students with clear goals and real motivation, not for those who are still figuring out direction at Year 12.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Jumeirah College Dubai's KHDA rating?

KHDA Outstanding — one of Dubai's strongest A-Level schools.

How much are fees at Jumeirah College Dubai?

Approximately AED 62,000–65,000 at Year 7, rising to AED 83,000–88,000 for Sixth Form A-Level (Year 12–13). Contact the school for confirmed current figures.

What are Jumeirah College's A-Level results?

Consistent 60–68% A*-A. Immediately below Dubai College (74–80%) and well ahead of most other Dubai British schools. Annual placements to UCL, King's College London, LSE, Cambridge and Manchester.

Can you enter Jumeirah College at Year 12?

Yes. Year 12 Sixth Form entry is available — GCSE results (typically Grade 7+ in 5 subjects) and school reference required. The most common Sixth Form transfer destination from Safa, JESS and DESS.

Is Jumeirah College a good transfer from Safa Community School?

Yes — it's the most common transfer route. Safa produces strong GCSE graduates; Jumeirah College's Year 12 entry accepts them on GCSE results. The Jumeirah location, similar school culture, and GCSE curriculum compatibility make this a natural, well-trodden path.
