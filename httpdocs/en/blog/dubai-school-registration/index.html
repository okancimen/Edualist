Dubai School Registration 2026: Step-by-Step Guide & Document Checklist

Quick Answer — What You Need for Dubai School Registration: Passport, visa, last 2 years' report cards (certified English translation), Transfer Certificate (authenticated via School → MEB → Apostille → UAE Consulate chain) and vaccination record. Start the application at least 12 months before your move — places at popular KHDA Outstanding schools fill very fast.

Every family moving to Dubai faces the same question: "Which school should I choose and where do I start?" Dubai has over 200 international schools, each with a different curriculum, fee range and admissions process. This guide walks you through the enrolment process from start to finish.

Özlem Çimen — Coach's note

Important: Dubai schools start in September–October. Places at popular schools can fill up 6–12 months in advance. Plan your move accordingly.

Understanding the Dubai School System

International schools in Dubai are regulated by KHDA (Knowledge and Human Development Authority). Every school is inspected annually and receives a rating — Outstanding, Good, Acceptable, etc. KHDA inspection reports are the most reliable source when shortlisting schools.

The main curriculum options in Dubai are: IB (International Baccalaureate), British (GCSE/A-Level), American (AP), French and Indian curricula. For most expat families, IB and British are the most common choices because both are globally recognised and ease school transitions internationally.

The Enrolment Process: 7 Steps

  1. 1
    Research schools on the KHDA website — At khda.ae you can filter by neighbourhood and curriculum. Each school's full inspection report is available there free of charge.
  2. 2
    Shortlist 3–5 schools and book tours — Most schools offer free tours. Bring your child; these visits are invaluable for assessing how they feel in the environment.
  3. 3
    Complete the application form and pay the fee — Application fees are typically 500–1,500 AED and are non-refundable. Make sure the school is a genuine fit before applying.
  4. 4
    Attend the assessment / placement test — Most schools conduct a short assessment to determine the right year group. This is a placement process, not an elimination test.
  5. 5
    Prepare your documents — Passport copy, visa, previous school transcripts (translated), vaccination record and passport photo. For documents from Turkey, see our Turkey-to-Dubai school transition document guide which explains the School → MEB → Apostille → UAE Consulate authentication chain step by step.
  6. 6
    Receive your offer letter and complete enrolment — After the offer letter, you typically have 1–2 weeks to pay the registration fee and secure the place. Do not miss this window.
  7. 7
    Plan the transition — Before school starts, talk to your child about the new system. Look into EAL (English as an Additional Language) support programmes if needed.

Dubai School Application Timelines 2026-27: When to Start by Curriculum

Application timing in Dubai varies significantly by curriculum and KHDA rating. Outstanding-rated IB and British schools have the tightest windows — 12–18 month waiting lists are the norm, not the exception. Applying after you have a confirmed relocation date is almost always too late for the most sought-after schools.

Application Timeline by Curriculum

IB schools (Outstanding) 18 months before target start
British schools (Outstanding) 12–18 months before start
Good / Very Good rated schools 6–9 months before start
Urgent relocation Apply immediately — parallel to your housing search
Waiting list reality: GEMS Wellington, Repton and JESS routinely have 12–18 month waiting lists for popular year groups. For Outstanding IB schools, waiting lists in early years can extend beyond 2 years. The safest approach: apply before your move date is confirmed.

Fees: Realistic Expectations

International school fees in Dubai range from 25,000 AED to 100,000+ AED per year. KHDA Outstanding-rated schools generally charge more. Many companies provide an education allowance for expat employees — ask your employer about this.

Scholarship opportunities: Some schools, particularly IB schools, offer scholarships for academic excellence or talent in arts and sport. Ask about scholarship programmes at the time of application.

The Waiting List Reality

Dubai's most sought-after KHDA Outstanding schools — GEMS Wellington, Repton Dubai, JESS — can have waiting lists of 12 to 18 months. This catches many families off guard. To find out which neighbourhood puts you closest to which schools, see our Dubai Neighbourhood Guide.

Joining a waiting list usually requires a non-refundable application fee. Ask the school directly how many families are ahead of you and what a realistic timeline looks like. My most consistent advice: be on multiple waiting lists simultaneously rather than betting on a single school.

Practical tip: If you're on a waiting list and haven't heard back, call the school every two months. Families who stay in touch get called earlier than those who go silent. Patience and follow-up are the two most critical ingredients in the Dubai school process.

EAL Support: What You Need to Know

The majority of international schools in Dubai offer EAL (English as an Additional Language) programmes — in-class support or individual sessions to help non-native English speakers develop their language skills alongside the mainstream curriculum.

EAL quality varies significantly between schools. KHDA inspection reports include a section assessing EAL provision — reading these is the most objective way to gauge how seriously a school takes language support. Before committing, ask whether EAL carries an additional fee and how many students currently receive it. Your child's first year depends greatly on the quality of this support. For age-by-age adaptation strategies, see our guide on helping children with limited English settle into Dubai schools.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Families most often make these mistakes: choosing a school based on reputation alone; underestimating their child's language readiness; leaving applications too late; applying to only one school. I always recommend applying to at least three schools simultaneously.

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for school registration in Dubai?

International school registration in Dubai requires: passport copy (student and parent), Emirates ID, visa or residence permit, previous school transcripts (certified translation into English), vaccination record and passport photograph.

When should I apply to Dubai schools?

The academic year starts in September. For popular KHDA Outstanding schools, you need to start the process by January–March of the previous year at the latest. For schools with waiting lists, plan 12–18 months ahead.

How do I use the KHDA portal to choose a school?

At khda.ae you can access inspection reports, rating histories and school profiles for all private schools in Dubai, free of charge. Use the search filters to create a shortlist by curriculum, neighbourhood and rating.

What happens when transferring between schools in Dubai?

You will need a Transfer Certificate (or School Leaving Certificate) from the current school. KHDA requires the departing school to be notified. The new school will ask for the previous school's transcript, conduct report and the Transfer Certificate.

Does KHDA control school fee increases?

Yes. KHDA monitors annual fee increases and only permits rises under specific conditions. Outstanding-rated schools are allowed higher increases; lower-rated schools face tighter limits.
