Dubai Special Education Guide: Dyslexia, ADHD, Autism and Gifted Children in Dubai Schools

Dubai SEN guide Dubai dyslexia school KHDA inclusive education Dubai ADHD support Dubai autism school

"My child has a diagnosis — will we find the right school in Dubai?" It is one of the first questions families with SEN children ask when considering a move. The reassuring answer: Dubai's SEN infrastructure is significantly more developed than many families expect, and KHDA schools are legally obligated to provide support.

The important caveat: the system works, but only if you know how to navigate it. The wrong school, disclosing at the wrong moment, or arriving without translated documents can cost time and cause unnecessary stress.

What KHDA Requires from Dubai Private Schools

Under the KHDA Inclusive Education Policy, every licensed private school in Dubai must:

  1. Make reasonable adjustments for students with Special Educational Needs (SEN)
  2. Appoint a Learning Support Coordinator (LSC/SENCO)
  3. Develop an individual Education Support Plan (IEP) for each SEN student

Critically: schools cannot legally refuse admission on SEN grounds. However, "we don't have the capacity" is a workaround some schools still use. If this happens, you have the right to escalate to KHDA directly. Schools' inclusive practices are evaluated in annual KHDA inspections — which is why well-rated schools invest in this area.

Four SEN Categories: Different Approaches for Each

🔵 Learning Difficulties: Dyslexia and Dyscalculia

Dyslexia (reading and writing difficulties) and dyscalculia (mathematics difficulties) are the most common SEN categories in Dubai schools. The vast majority of schools have structured support for these learners.

  • Small group literacy/numeracy sessions (typically 3–5 times per week)
  • Permission to use assistive technology (tablet, text-to-speech software)
  • Exam accommodations: extended time, separate room, reader, scribe
  • IB exam accommodations are also possible (requires IBO approval)

🟡 Attention Difficulties: ADHD / ADD

ADHD is well-recognised in Dubai. Most schools have LSC staff with specific ADHD training. If your child is on medication, note that ADHD medications are classified as controlled substances in the UAE — a school notification letter and sometimes an import permit from the Ministry of Health are required. Clarify this before enrolment.

  • Preferential seating (front row, low-distraction setting)
  • Task chunking, written instructions alongside verbal
  • Exam time extensions and separate exam room
  • Medication management via school nurse (DHA protocol)

🟢 Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)

ASD is where school selection matters most — support capacity varies enormously between schools. Mild-to-moderate spectrum: mainstream classroom with LSC support. Higher support needs: may require a dedicated inclusion unit or specialist school. KHDA has authorised certain schools specifically for ASD provision.

  • Noise-reduction headphones, predictable routine, visual schedules
  • Sensory break room / quiet space provision
  • Social skills groups (1–2 sessions per week at some schools)
  • 1:1 shadow teacher — usually at additional cost (see below)

⭐ Gifted and Talented

KHDA classifies gifted learners under the SEN umbrella and expects schools to provide differentiated instruction. American curriculum schools typically run a GATE (Gifted and Talented Education) programme. IB schools, by design, offer strong stretch and enrichment opportunities at every level.

  • Accelerated curriculum / grade skip (rare but possible)
  • Enrichment projects, independent research
  • Early exposure to IB Higher Level content (in MYP)
  • Guidance toward external competitions and programmes

Using an Overseas Diagnosis in Dubai

Diagnosis reports from overseas specialists — educational psychologists, psychiatrists, paediatricians — can be submitted to Dubai schools. How schools treat them varies:

  • Best case: The school uses the report as a reference, conducts its own brief assessment, and builds an IEP directly.
  • Common case: The school requests a Dubai-based re-evaluation (AED 2,500–5,000 with a local educational psychologist or neuropsychologist).
  • Practical advice: Always bring an English certified translation of the report. Many schools will accept a notarised translation; some ask for apostille — confirm before you travel.

If you want a Dubai re-evaluation, look for US- or UK-trained psychologists or neuropsychologists. KHDA-approved assessment centres are also available.

Timing Your Disclosure

This is the question most families find awkward. The honest answer:

You are not legally required to disclose at the application stage. The strategic approach most families find effective: apply through the standard process, receive your acceptance, then request a meeting with the school's LSC/SENCO before formally enrolling.

That said, early disclosure has genuine advantages for children with higher support needs (significant ASD, complex learning profiles). It gives the school time to prepare, and you get to gauge how the school responds — their reaction to your disclosure is itself valuable information about how they will treat your child. A good opening question: "Can you walk me through how you typically support a child with [dyslexia / ADHD / ASD] from day one?"

Schools with Strong SEN Provision

Most KHDA Outstanding-rated schools maintain capable LSC teams. Schools specifically noted for SEN provision:

  • GEMS Wellington Academy (DSO and Al Khail) — Comprehensive learning support department; strong track record with dyslexia and ADHD
  • Greenfield Community School — IB school; well-regarded inclusion policy
  • Repton School Dubai — Strong SENCO team; British curriculum
  • Brighton College Dubai — Active LSC programme; accessible fees relative to peers
  • Dubai British School (DBS) — British curriculum; established LSC provision
  • Swiss International Scientific School — IB; inclusive admissions policy
  • American School of Dubai (ASD) — GATE programme; strongest option for gifted learners
  • Raffles World Academy — IB; smaller class sizes; personal attention

For ASD with higher support needs, ask the school directly whether they have a dedicated inclusion unit — this provision changes year to year and must be confirmed at time of application.

  • Diagnosis report (original + certified English translation)

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Dubai private schools required to support children with SEN?

Yes. Under KHDA's Inclusive Education Policy, all licensed private schools in Dubai must make reasonable adjustments for students with special educational needs and appoint a Learning Support Coordinator (LSC/SENCO). Schools cannot legally refuse admission solely on the grounds of a SEN diagnosis.

Is a diagnosis report from overseas valid in Dubai schools?

Overseas diagnosis reports can be submitted to Dubai schools as a reference. Have the report officially translated into English. Schools may use it to initiate their own assessment or build an IEP directly. Some schools accept the overseas report at face value; others request a re-evaluation by a Dubai-based specialist (AED 2,500–5,000).

Should I disclose my child's diagnosis during the school application?

You are not legally obliged to disclose at the application stage. The recommended strategy: apply normally, receive the acceptance, then request a meeting with the school's LSC/SENCO before formally enrolling. For children with more complex needs (ASD, significant learning difficulties), early disclosure allows the school to prepare properly and prevents surprises on both sides.

What does learning support cost in Dubai schools?

Basic learning support is typically included in school tuition. Individual 1:1 sessions cost AED 150–350 per session (30–45 min). Three sessions per week adds approximately AED 12,000–18,000 per year. A full-time shadow teacher for ASD costs AED 25,000–50,000 per year.

Which Dubai schools have the strongest learning support provision?

GEMS Wellington, Repton School Dubai, Greenfield Community School, Brighton College Dubai, and Dubai British School are frequently cited for strong SEN provision. For gifted learners, the American School of Dubai offers a GATE programme. For ASD with higher support needs, verify directly with the school and KHDA whether a dedicated inclusion unit is available.
