  • Curriculum: British National Curriculum + IGCSE + IB Diploma / A-Level (Al Barsha)

Summary

Kings' School Dubai was founded in 1998 at Umm Hurair and is one of Dubai's most established British schools. Two KHDA Outstanding campuses, a strong community culture and the highest referral rate among expat families are its distinguishing marks. Its smaller, more personal atmosphere — compared to larger schools like JESS or Repton — makes it a particularly compelling choice for families new to international schooling. This review brings together KHDA data, a 2026–27 fee table, parent reviews from multiple platforms and a coaching perspective.

Why community culture matters more than it sounds. For a family just relocated to Dubai, a school's "warmth" can sound abstract — but in my coaching experience, its effects are concrete. Being able to reach your child's teacher by phone, knowing that other parents know each other, and having another expat parent show you the ropes at pick-up: these things shorten the adaptation curve significantly. Kings' creates this systematically. Most of its larger competitors do not.

Coaching Perspective: Who Is Kings' Right For?

The family profile I recommend Kings' for: families arriving in Dubai who want their child to settle in a warm, personal and supportive environment first. Especially compelling for children making their first international school transition, children whose English is still developing, or families who want to avoid the impersonal atmosphere of larger institutional schools.

Where I'm more cautious: families seeking an intensely competitive academic culture (for that, Dubai College or Repton are stronger fits), families who prioritise extensive sports facilities or a wide variety of ECA programmes, and families seeking the very strongest IB / A-Level cohort for Post-16 (Al Barsha covers this, but note the smaller cohort size).

Three Things That Make Kings' Distinctive

  • Established community culture: The parent community built since 1998 actively supports newly arriving families. Quick network-building, practical knowledge-sharing and in-school peer support — this is part of the Kings' experience in a way it simply isn't at most competitors.
  • Accessible fees, Outstanding quality: One of the few schools offering KHDA Outstanding in the AED 60,000–75,000 primary band. Repton and Dubai College carry the same quality label at a materially higher cost.
  • Pastoral care leadership: Outstanding in Student Wellbeing — a category most large schools only reach Very Good. Kings' supports this structurally: teacher familiarity, small class groups and a strong pastoral system.

The original Umm Hurair campus is strong in primary education and carries the school's historical community. However, children leaving at Year 6 will need to transfer to Al Barsha or another school — plan this transition in advance. If your child is starting at age 3 and you want continuity to 18, Al Barsha offers a more complete single-school journey from the outset.

Notes for Expat Families

In my consulting practice, Kings' is among the schools I hear about most from families who have just arrived in Dubai. "There were expat families there to help us from day one" is a comment I've heard many times. This is not coincidence — the school's community structure has systematically created a culture that integrates new arrivals.

EAL support is strong. That said, objectively assessing your child's academic English level before entry remains important. Kings' supports EAL but assumes foundational academic literacy. A CAT4 assessment provides a useful baseline before the admissions process.

Location note: Umm Hurair sits in the Bur Dubai / Oud Metha corridor — factor in a morning commute from Jumeirah or Marina. Al Barsha is centrally positioned with broader accessibility.

Admissions

  • Application channel: Kings Academies online portal at kingsacademies.ae
  • Assessment: Age-appropriate academic test; Early Years (EYFS/KS1) use informal observation and play-based assessment
  • Secondary entry: Academic test + school references; Year 7+ includes English and Maths proficiency assessment
  • Waiting lists: Popular year groups (particularly Foundation and Year 1–3) may have waiting lists; contact admissions@kingsacademies.ae for current availability
  • Application timing: Enrolment openings typically begin January–March of the preceding academic year

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kings' School Dubai's KHDA rating?

Both campuses (Umm Hurair and Al Barsha) hold KHDA Outstanding ratings. Student Wellbeing and Personal Development — two areas where many larger schools only reach Very Good — are also Outstanding at Kings'.

How much are fees at Kings' School Dubai in 2026–27?

Approximately AED 54,440 (Foundation Stage 1) to AED 88,000–89,500 (Year 12–13 IB / A-Level at Al Barsha). Primary band is approximately AED 57,000–68,000 and secondary AED 72,000–79,000. Contact the school directly for current fees.

What is the difference between Kings' Dubai and Kings' Al Barsha?

Umm Hurair is a primary school (Foundation – Year 6). Year 6 leavers need to transfer to another school. Al Barsha runs from Foundation through Year 13 and offers IGCSE, A-Level and IB Diploma. Both campuses are KHDA Outstanding.

Does Kings' School Dubai offer the IB Diploma?

Yes — Kings' School Al Barsha offers both IB Diploma and A-Level at Post-16. The primary-only Umm Hurair campus does not offer these programmes.

Why is Kings' particularly popular with expat families?

An established expat parent community provides fast adaptation support to newly arriving families. The school's community atmosphere, high teacher retention, strong EAL support and Outstanding quality at a more accessible fee range make Kings' one of the most frequently recommended schools for expat families — particularly those from Turkey and the Middle East.

How do I apply to Kings' School Dubai?

Via the Kings Academies online portal at kingsacademies.ae. Popular year groups may have waiting lists — contact admissions@kingsacademies.ae for current availability.

How do parent satisfaction scores compare?

4.2/5 on WhichSchoolAdvisor with 86% recommendation rate. Teacher quality and pastoral care score particularly high. Key positives: community warmth, teacher retention, EAL support. Points to note: Umm Hurair's primary-only structure requires a Year 6 transfer.
