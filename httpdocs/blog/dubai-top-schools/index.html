Top 5 Schools in Dubai — Coaching Notes

#1 — JESS (Jumeirah English Speaking School)

Best for: families wanting EYFS-through-sixth-form continuity in the British system, and the broadest expat community in Dubai.

JESS is the most frequently asked-about school among the families I coach. Both the Umm Suqeim and Arabian Ranches campuses are KHDA Outstanding. The school's strength is consistency: strong academic results, well-resourced pastoral support and a genuinely international student body. High demand means waiting lists fill up 12–18 months out — if JESS is on your list, register interest early.

#2 — Dubai International Academy (DIA) Emirates Hills

Best for: families with a clear global university target and a child who thrives in an inquiry-based, project-heavy environment.

DIA is Dubai's only school offering the complete IB continuum — PYP in primary, MYP in middle school and IB Diploma at the top. University placement results are among the best in the UAE. At 80,000–92,000 AED per year it sits at the premium end, but for families targeting Russell Group, Ivy League or top European universities, DIA is the natural first choice.

#3 — Repton School Dubai

Best for: families seeking the academic rigour and traditions of a top British independent school, from Year 3 upward.

Repton in the UK was founded in 1557. Its Dubai campus brings the same boarding-school academic culture — competitive A-Level results, strong sports and arts programmes, and smaller class sizes than many GEMS schools. Secondary-focused; if your child is in EYFS or KS1 you'll need a primary school first and a secondary transfer plan.

#4 — GEMS Wellington International School

Best for: families who want a large, fully resourced campus with a wide extra-curricular offering and a long track record in Dubai.

Wellington International is the flagship of the GEMS group — Dubai's largest school operator. The Al Sufouh campus has extensive facilities and consistent IGCSE/A-Level results. It is one of the first names expat families hear when they arrive in Dubai, which means it is also one of the busiest.

#5 — Nord Anglia International School (NAIS)

Best for: families who value whole-child development — arts, music, sport and academic achievement — and an international network that goes beyond Dubai.

Nord Anglia is a global group with 80+ schools worldwide. Its Jumeirah Park campus offers a blend of British curriculum and IB Diploma at sixth form. What sets it apart: exclusive partnerships with MIT (STEM enrichment), Juilliard (performing arts) and Chelsea FC Foundation (sport). A strong choice for creatively minded or multidisciplinary students.

#6–#10 — The Rest of the Top 10

#6 Dubai CollegeSecondary only (Y7–Y13). Dubai's most academically selective school — consistently the highest A-Level results in the UAE. Small intake, competitive assessment for entry. Right for motivated, academically strong teenagers.
#7 Kings School DubaiBoth the Dubai and Al Barsha campuses are Outstanding. EYFS through Year 11; IB Diploma available at 6th form. Known for a warm community culture — Turkish and expat families consistently mention the welcoming atmosphere. More affordable than the top 5.
#8 GEMS Dubai American Academy (DAA)The only Outstanding-rated American curriculum school in Dubai. Strong AP programme, SAT preparation and direct pathways to US and Canadian universities. Essential choice if North America is the university destination. For UK or global targets, IB or British is stronger.
#9 Safa Community SchoolSmaller than most Outstanding schools, which is a deliberate strength — teachers know every child. Strong balance of academic results and student wellbeing. Jumeirah location. One of the best value Outstanding schools in Dubai.
#10 DESS + DESCDubai English Speaking School (primary) and Dubai English Speaking College (secondary) form a seamless UK pathway. KHDA Outstanding, community-focused, and among the most affordable Outstanding schools in Dubai — a genuine hidden gem.

What About the Top 20 Schools in Dubai?

Dubai has 23 KHDA Outstanding schools in total — so "top 20" and "top 23" are essentially the same list. Schools #11–23 include JESS Arabian Ranches (a second JESS campus), GEMS Jumeira Primary School, Victory Heights Primary, Dubai British School (Emirates Hills & Jumeirah Park), Deira International School, Horizons English School, and the French and Indian curriculum Outstanding schools.

How to Use This Ranking

No ranking replaces a school visit. Use this table to build your initial shortlist of 3–5 schools, then:

  • Confirm your child's English level meets each school's entry requirements
  • Check waiting list status — Outstanding schools fill fast
  • Visit each campus before submitting an application
  • Apply to at least 3 schools simultaneously
  • Factor in your neighbourhood — Dubai traffic makes a 15-minute commute worth choosing over a 5-star school 40 minutes away
Özlem Çimen — Coach's note

I moved to Dubai with my own child and went through exactly this process. I started with the list — then visited schools, joined waiting lists, and ultimately changed my initial choice when an unexpected offer came through. The lesson: treat this ranking as a compass, not a verdict. The best school for your child depends on details no ranking can capture. That's what the first consultation is for.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the top 10 schools in Dubai?

Based on KHDA Outstanding ratings: (1) JESS, (2) Dubai International Academy, (3) Repton School Dubai, (4) GEMS Wellington International, (5) Nord Anglia International School, (6) Dubai College, (7) Kings School Dubai, (8) GEMS Dubai American Academy, (9) Safa Community School, (10) DESS/DESC. All hold Outstanding status from KHDA, Dubai's official school inspection body.

What is the number 1 school in Dubai?

There is no single "number 1" — the best school depends on your child's age, target curriculum and family situation. For IB and global universities: DIA. For British curriculum continuity: JESS or Repton. For the American curriculum: GEMS DAA. All are KHDA Outstanding.

What is the best IB school in Dubai?

Dubai International Academy (DIA) is Dubai's best IB school — it is the only one offering the full PYP + MYP + IB Diploma continuum and has Outstanding status. GEMS World Academy and Nord Anglia also offer strong IB Diploma programmes, both Outstanding-rated.

How much do the top 10 schools in Dubai cost?

Annual fees range from ~48,000 AED (DESS/DESC) to ~92,000 AED (DIA). Most Outstanding schools charge 65,000–85,000 AED per year. Many companies provide education allowances of 80,000–120,000 AED for expat employees in Dubai.

How long are waiting lists at top Dubai schools?

Most Outstanding schools have 1–3 year waiting lists. Apply to JESS, Repton and GEMS Wellington at least 12–18 months in advance. Submit to 3+ schools simultaneously — this is the most reliable way to secure a place at a top school.

Which school in Dubai has the best A-Level results?

Dubai College consistently produces Dubai's highest A-Level results. Repton School Dubai and GEMS Wellington International are also among the top performers. All three are KHDA Outstanding and follow the British curriculum.

Not sure which school fits your child?

Every family's situation is different — child's age, English level, target university, budget and neighbourhood all shape the right answer. Book a free first session to work through your specific shortlist.

En İyi 5 Okul — Koçtan Notlar

#1 — JESS (Jumeirah English Speaking School)

İdeal: EYFS'den A-Level'e kesintisiz British sistemini ve Dubai'nin en köklü expat topluluğunu isteyen aileler için.

JESS, danıştığım ailelerin en sık sorduğu okul. Her iki kampüs (Umm Suqeim ve Arabian Ranches) de KHDA Outstanding. Güçlü yanı tutarlılık: güçlü akademik sonuçlar, iyi yapılandırılmış rehberlik ve gerçekten uluslararası öğrenci profili. Yüksek talep nedeniyle bekleme listesi 12–18 ay öncesinde başlıyor — JESS listenizde varsa hemen kayıt başlatın.

#2 — Dubai International Academy (DIA)

İdeal: Küresel üniversite hedefi net olan ve araştırma temelli öğrenme ortamında gelişen çocuklar için.

DIA, Dubai'nin tam IB zinciri sunan tek okulu: PYP (primary), MYP (orta) ve IB Diploma. Üniversite yerleştirme sonuçları BAE'nin en iyileri arasında. Yıllık 80.000–92.000 AED ile premium fiyat aralığında — ama Russell Group, Ivy League veya üst Avrupa üniversiteleri hedefliyorsanız DIA birinci tercih.

#3 — Repton School Dubai

İdeal: İngiliz bağımsız okul akademik kültürünü ve geleneklerini arayan, Y3 ve üzeri çocuklar için.

Repton İngiltere'de 1557'de kuruldu. Dubai kampüsü aynı boardingschool akademik kültürünü taşıyor — rekabetçi A-Level sonuçları, güçlü spor ve sanat programları, küçük sınıf büyüklükleri. Secondary odaklı; EYFS/KS1 yaşındaki çocuklar için önce ayrı bir primary okul gerekir.

#4 — GEMS Wellington International School

İdeal: Kapsamlı tesislere, geniş ekstra müfredata ve Dubai'de uzun geçmişe sahip büyük bir kampüs isteyen aileler için.

Wellington International, Dubai'nin en büyük okul operatörü GEMS'in amiral gemisi. Al Sufouh kampüsü geniş altyapıya ve tutarlı IGCSE/A-Level sonuçlarına sahip. Dubai'ye yeni gelen expat ailelerin ilk duyduğu isimlerden biri — yani en dolulardan biri de.

#5 — Nord Anglia International School (NAIS)

İdeal: Sanat, müzik, spor ve akademiyi dengeleyen, bütünsel gelişimi önceleyen aileler için.

Nord Anglia, 80+ okullu küresel bir eğitim grubu. Jumeirah Park kampüsü British müfredat + IB Diploma sunuyor. Farkı: MIT (STEM zenginleştirme), Juilliard (sahne sanatları) ve Chelsea FC Foundation (spor) işbirlikleri. Yaratıcı ya da çok yönlü gelişim arayan çocuklar için güçlü seçenek.

#6–#10 — Top 10'un Geri Kalanı

#6 Dubai CollegeYalnızca secondary (Y7–Y13). Dubai'nin akademik seçimde en rekabetçi okulu — BAE'nin en yüksek A-Level sonuçları. Küçük kontenjan, giriş değerlendirmesiyle seçim yapıyor. Akademik olarak güçlü, üniversiteye odaklı gençler için.
#7 Kings School DubaiHer iki kampüs (Dubai ve Al Barsha) Outstanding. EYFS'den Y11'e; 6. sınıfta IB Diploma seçeneği var. Sıcak topluluk kültürüyle biliniyor — Türk aileler bu konuda bizzat söylemişlerdir. Top 5'ten daha uygun fiyatlı.
#8 GEMS Dubai American Academy (DAA)Dubai'deki tek Outstanding Amerikan müfredatı okulu. Güçlü AP programı ve SAT hazırlığı; ABD/Kanada üniversiteleri için doğal yol. İngiltere veya küresel hedef varsa IB veya British daha güçlü.
#9 Safa Community SchoolÇoğu Outstanding okuldan daha küçük — bu bilinçli bir güç. Öğretmenler her öğrenciyi tanır. Güçlü akademik/refah dengesi. Jumeirah. Dubai'nin en iyi değer-kalite dengelerinden biri.
#10 DESS + DESCDubai English Speaking School (primary) + College (secondary) birlikte kesintisiz UK yolu sunuyor. KHDA Outstanding, topluluk odaklı ve Dubai'nin en uygun fiyatlı Outstanding okullarından — gerçek bir gizli mücevher.

Sık Sorulan Sorular

Dubai'de en iyi 10 okul hangileri?

KHDA Outstanding derecesine göre: (1) JESS, (2) Dubai International Academy, (3) Repton School Dubai, (4) GEMS Wellington International, (5) Nord Anglia International School, (6) Dubai College, (7) Kings School Dubai, (8) GEMS Dubai American Academy, (9) Safa Community School, (10) DESS/DESC. Tümü KHDA Outstanding.

Dubai'nin 1 numaralı okulu hangisi?

Tek bir "1 numara" yok — en iyi okul çocuğun yaşına, hedef müfredatına ve aile bütçesine göre değişir. IB ve küresel üniversiteler için DIA; British müfredatı için JESS veya Repton; Amerikan müfredatı için GEMS DAA öne çıkıyor. Tümü KHDA Outstanding.

Dubai'de en iyi IB okulu hangisi?

Dubai International Academy (DIA), Dubai'nin en iyi IB okulu — PYP + MYP + IB Diploma'yı sunan tek okul ve Outstanding dereceli. GEMS World Academy ve Nord Anglia da güçlü IB Diploma programları sunuyor.

Dubai'nin en iyi 10 okulu ne kadar ücret alıyor?

Yıllık ücretler ~48.000 AED (DESS/DESC) ile ~92.000 AED (DIA) arasında değişiyor. Çoğu Outstanding okul yılda 65.000–85.000 AED aralığında. Pek çok şirket Dubai'deki expat çalışanlara 80.000–120.000 AED/yıl eğitim yardımı sağlıyor.

En iyi okullarda bekleme listeleri ne kadar?

Outstanding okulların büyük çoğunluğunda 1–3 yıl bekleme listesi var. JESS, Repton ve GEMS Wellington için en az 12–18 ay öncesinden başvurun. Birden fazla okula eş zamanlı başvurmak en güvenli yol.

Hangi okul çocuğunuza uygun?

Her ailenin durumu farklı — çocuğun yaşı, İngilizce seviyesi, hedef üniversite, bütçe ve semt doğru cevabı şekillendiriyor. Kişisel okul listenizi birlikte oluşturalım.
