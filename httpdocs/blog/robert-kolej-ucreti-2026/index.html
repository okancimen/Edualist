Gerçek Maliyet Farkı: Neden Rakamlar Yanıltıcı Olabilir?

🇹🇷 Türkiye — Gerçek Maliyet Faktörleri
  • Yıllık ücretler TL bazlı — yüksek enflasyonla birlikte her yıl %40-70 artış riski
  • Bu okullara girebilmek için önce pahalı dershane/hazırlık süreci
  • Brüt gelirden ödeme: vergi sonrası net gelirden daha fazla kazanmak gerekiyor
  • 5 yıllık toplam maliyet tahmin edilemez
🇦🇪 Dubai — Gerçek Maliyet Faktörleri
  • Ücretler AED bazlı — Türkiye enflasyonundan korunaklı
  • Gelir vergisi yok: net maaş = brüt maaş
  • KHDA ücret artışlarını denetliyor — sürpriz artış yok
  • Giriş sınavı yok — dershane masrafı yok

Robert Kolej Fee 2026-27: The Number

Robert Kolej's annual tuition: 2,488,200 TL — covering tuition only. Boarding, transport and meals add an estimated 500,000–700,000 TL annually for some students. Founded in 1863, fully IB Diploma, and Turkey's most selective school by admission criteria. The price reflects real academic value. But context matters.

What Does the Same Budget Buy in Dubai?

At 1 AED = 12.65 TL, Dubai's KHDA Outstanding IB school Dubai International Academy (DIA) costs approximately 1,012,000–1,202,000 TL annually — less than half of Robert Kolej. Robert Kolej's annual fee covers DIA for two full years. For a detailed breakdown see our Turkey vs Dubai school fees comparison.

The Real Cost Gap

Fee tables alone are misleading. Turkey's TL-based fees face 40-70% annual inflation increases, require pre-school exam prep costs, and are paid from taxed income. Dubai's AED-based fees are inflation-shielded, KHDA-regulated, and paid from zero-income-tax salaries. The five-year total cost differential is far larger than the single-year comparison suggests.

Is there a Dubai school equivalent to Robert Kolej?

Dubai International Academy (DIA) offers the full IB Diploma, holds KHDA Outstanding status, and costs approximately 1,012,000–1,202,000 TL annually — less than half of Robert Kolej. No entrance exam required.

How fast do Turkish private school fees increase?

Over the past three years, annual increases have averaged 40-70%, tracking Turkish inflation. KHDA in Dubai caps fee increases for international schools — a structural protection Turkish families don't have.
